Feature: Shopping Cart

As a logged-in customer,
I want to add and remove items from my shopping cart,
So that I can manage my selections before checking out.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Adding a product increments the cart badge count by 1
- Adding two different products shows a badge count of 2
- Removing a product decrements the badge count
- Removing the last item from the cart hides the cart badge entirely
- The cart page shows the correct items after navigating to it
